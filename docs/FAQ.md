# ❓ StoreWise Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### Q: What is StoreWise?
**A:** StoreWise is a comprehensive Point of Sale (POS) and Inventory Management system designed for minimarkets and retail businesses. It helps you manage sales, track inventory, handle customer relationships, and generate business reports.

### Q: What are the system requirements?
**A:**
- **Browser**: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
- **Internet**: Stable connection for real-time features
- **Screen**: Minimum 1024x768 resolution
- **Device**: Desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone

### Q: Is my data secure?
**A:** Yes! We use bank-level encryption, secure data centers, regular security audits, and comply with data protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

## Account & Access

### Q: How do I reset my password?
**A:**
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your email for password reset link
4. Create a new strong password
5. Login with your new password

### Q: I can't log in. What should I do?
**A:** Try these steps in order:
1. Verify correct email and password
2. Check if Caps Lock is on
3. Clear your browser cache and cookies
4. Try a different browser
5. Contact your administrator if issues persist

### Q: How do I change my user information?
**A:**
1. Click on your profile avatar in the top-right corner
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown
3. Update your information
4. Click "Save Changes"

## Point of Sale (POS)

### Q: How do I add a product to a sale?
**A:** You have several options:
- **Search**: Type the product name or SKU in the search bar
- **Browse**: Use category filters to find products
- **Scan**: Use a barcode scanner to scan product barcodes
- **Click**: Simply click on any product in the grid to add it

### Q: Can I modify items in the cart?
**A:** Yes! Use the `+` and `-` buttons to adjust quantities, or click the trash icon to remove items entirely.

### Q: What payment methods are supported?
**A:** StoreWise supports:
- Cash
- Credit/Debit Cards
- Mobile Payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- Bank Transfers
- Store Credit/Account

### Q: How do I issue a refund?
**A:**
1. Go to Sales → Sales History
2. Find the transaction you want to refund
3. Click "Refund" next to the transaction
4. Select items to refund and reason
5. Process the refund through the original payment method

### Q: Can I put a sale on hold?
**A:** Yes! Click the "Hold Sale" button in the cart. You can retrieve held sales later from the POS screen.

## Inventory Management

### Q: How do I add a new product?
**A:**
1. Go to Products → Add Product
2. Fill in product information (name, SKU, price, etc.)
3. Set initial stock quantity
4. Add product images if desired
5. Click "Save Product"

### Q: What's the difference between Cost Price and Selling Price?
**A:**
- **Cost Price**: What you pay to acquire the product
- **Selling Price**: What customers pay for the product
- **Profit**: Selling Price minus Cost Price

### Q: How do I handle low stock alerts?
**A:**
1. Go to Inventory → Low Stock Alerts
2. Review products that need restocking
3. Create purchase orders or adjust stock levels
4. Set up automatic reorder points for future alerts

### Q: Can I track batches and expiry dates?
**A:** Yes! StoreWise supports:
- Batch/lot number tracking
- Expiration date monitoring
- First-expire-first-out (FEFO) inventory rotation
- Automated expiry alerts

### Q: How do I do stock adjustments?
**A:**
1. Go to Inventory → Stock Adjustments
2. Click "New Adjustment"
3. Select products and quantities to adjust
4. Choose adjustment reason (damage, loss, etc.)
5. Submit for manager approval

## Customer Management

### Q: How do I add a new customer?
**A:**
1. Go to Customers → Add Customer
2. Enter customer information (name, phone, email)
3. Add optional details like address and birthday
4. Assign loyalty tier if applicable
5. Click "Save Customer"

### Q: What are loyalty points?
**A:** Loyalty points are rewards customers earn with purchases. They can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases.

### Q: How do customers earn points?
**A:** Customers earn points based on:
- Purchase amount (typically 1 point per $1 spent)
- Special promotions and bonuses
- Referral programs
- Birthday rewards

### Q: Can I view a customer's purchase history?
**A:** Yes! Go to Customers → Select Customer → Purchase History to see all past transactions.

### Q: How do customer tiers work?
**A:** Customer tiers provide increasing benefits:
- **Bronze**: Basic benefits
- **Silver**: Better discounts and point multipliers
- **Gold**: Premium benefits and exclusive offers
- **Platinum**: VIP treatment and highest rewards

## Reports & Analytics

### Q: What reports are available?
**A:** StoreWise offers comprehensive reports:
- **Sales Reports**: Daily, weekly, monthly sales data
- **Inventory Reports**: Stock levels, movements, valuations
- **Customer Reports**: Analytics, loyalty data, demographics
- **Financial Reports**: Profit & loss, tax reports
- **Staff Performance**: Sales by employee

### Q: How do I generate a report?
**A:**
1. Go to Reports → Select report type
2. Choose date range and filters
3. Click "Generate Report"
4. View online or export to PDF/Excel

### Q: Can I schedule automatic reports?
**A:** Yes! You can schedule reports to be automatically:
- Generated daily, weekly, or monthly
- Emailed to specified recipients
- Saved to your report archive

### Q: How do I export data?
**A:** Most reports support export to:
- PDF (for printing and sharing)
- Excel (for further analysis)
- CSV (for data processing)

## Technical Support

### Q: The system is running slow. What can I do?
**A:** Try these troubleshooting steps:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Close unused browser tabs
3. Check your internet connection speed
4. Restart your browser or device
5. Contact support if issues persist

### Q: I'm having trouble with barcode scanning. Help!
**A:**
1. Check barcode scanner connection
2. Ensure scanner is in correct mode
3. Verify barcode quality on products
4. Check lighting conditions
5. Try manual entry as backup

### Q: Can I use StoreWise on mobile devices?
**A:** Yes! StoreWise is fully responsive and works on:
- Smartphones (iOS and Android)
- Tablets (iPad, Android tablets)
- Mobile browsers

### Q: Is there an offline mode?
**A:** While StoreWise requires internet for real-time features, it can cache essential data for limited offline functionality. However, we recommend maintaining an internet connection for full functionality.
